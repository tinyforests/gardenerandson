Common questions

How Tend works in practice.

How does the initial visit work?

Your steward visits, walks the garden with you, records the existing species, assesses the soil, and identifies the ecological vegetation class. From this baseline we set the seasonal calendar and start the field notes.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly subscribers can cancel at any time — your subscription runs to the end of the current billing month, with no further visits scheduled after that.

What happens if I cancel an annual subscription early?

The annual rate is a discount for paying upfront. If you cancel partway through, we charge the standard monthly rate for visits already delivered and refund the difference. No further penalty.

Can I pause my subscription?

Grove and Canopy subscribers can pause for up to 8 weeks per year at no charge — useful for winter, travel, or renovations. Billing pauses with the service; your subscription end-date moves forward by the pause duration.

Is mulch included?

No. Mulch quality varies enormously by property and we'd rather quote honestly. Each subscriber receives a separate mulch quote when needed.

Who comes to my garden?

A dedicated steward who knows your block. Continuity matters — the field notes only build value if the same eyes return to read them.

What's the Ecological Registry?

A public-record-style platform Gardener & Son maintains, tracking ecological condition across enrolled gardens. Grove and Canopy subscribers' species observations are recorded there as part of the service.
