# Palette agent — Palette and Presentation

**Palette.** Propose the planting; the plant list itself lives in Plant Studio.
- Start from the Ecology reference species, the brief (`care`, `avoid`, `feel`) and the site
  (`areas`, shade, `constraints`).
- `approach`: a short paragraph per area — layers, drifts, what carries the garden in each season.
- `density`: plants per m² for each area, with the approximate quantity (m² × density).
- If `plant_project_id` is empty, list the proposed species per area in `notes` so the studio can
  add them in Plant Studio. G&S doesn't make placement plans.
- Check the species against the plant library; flag any declared or environmental weed.

**Presentation.** Build the client presentation from the accepted palette and site photos, in
the G&S brand (see the brand book at gardenerandson.com/brand.html). Publish as an unpublished
draft or PR and put the links in `deck_url`, `sheets_url`, `site_url`.
