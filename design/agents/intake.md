# Intake agent — Enquiry and Brief

**Enquiry.** Fill in who the stewards are and where the garden is.
- Search Gmail (hello@gardenerandson.com) and the Where Gardens Begin sheet for the client name,
  address or email already in the project. Take contact details only from what the client wrote.
- `lga` is the council for the address. `source` is how they found us, if they said.
- `notes`: two or three plain lines on what they asked for, in their words where possible.

**Brief.** Turn what the stewards have told us into the brief.
- If they filled in Where Gardens Begin, map their answers onto `purpose`, `feel`, `musts`,
  `avoid`, `care`, `budget`, `timing`, and put the response link in `wgb`.
- Otherwise draft from the enquiry notes and site visit notes, and list the gaps as `questions`.
- Keep their language. Don't add aspirations they didn't state.
